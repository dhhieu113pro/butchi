Privacy

Local processing

Butchi processes selected text with the GGUF model configured on your device. Selected text and generated results are not sent to a cloud AI service by Butchi.

Local history

Translate and Rewrite history is stored locally on your device and can be cleared from Settings.

Network use

Butchi uses network access when you explicitly download a model from Hugging Face. After a model is downloaded, inference runs locally.
