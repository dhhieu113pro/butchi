Control without clutter

Local AI, configured your way.

Butchi keeps common controls easy to reach while exposing deeper inference settings only when you need them.

  • Translate and Rewrite preferences
  • Editable prompt profiles
  • Local GGUF model download and load
  • Auto, GPU, or CPU preference
  • Searchable local history
  • System, light, and dark appearance

Your text is not sent to a cloud AI service.

Selected text and generated results are processed locally by your configured GGUF model. History stays on your device.

Network access is used when you explicitly download a model from Hugging Face.
